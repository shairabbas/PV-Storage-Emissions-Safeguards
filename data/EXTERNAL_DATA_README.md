# Public input provenance and boundaries

The EIA-930 `ERCO` regional profile is assembled from the official [six-month balancing-authority CSVs](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/gridmonitor/) for 2022--2024 and the official [ERCO hourly workbook](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/gridmonitor/knownissues/xls/ERCO.xlsx). Input URLs and development-file SHA-256 digests are in `../audit/eia_provenance.json`; final input digests are recorded in the final manifest. See [EIA-930 methodology](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/930-content/about.html).

The regional demand, solar and wind values are EIA-reported balancing-area data. The hourly production CO2 values are **EIA estimates** in the workbook. Neither source observes what generators would have done under this project's battery intervention. The community load and PV cases are scaled regional shapes, not participant measurements. A 2022-fitted observational regression supplies a **model-generated** marginal response; its 2023 error is in `../audit/eia_response_model.json`. Average production intensity enters the attributional carbon ledger and the import carbon charge in applicable scheduling objectives; it is **not** used as the consequential marginal response. The electricity tariff is synthetic.

**Version-3 correction.** The inherited regional frame multiplied both the average production factor and scaled production-emissions baseline by 1000. Since numeric tonnes/MWh equal kg/kWh, the corrected factor is `estimated_CO2_tonnes / demand_MW`, and the scaled baseline is `0.2 * estimated_CO2_tonnes` kg. The fitted marginal derivative and curvature were already in their intended units. Corrected frames have `_corrected.csv.gz` suffixes; all 15 policies in both the 2023 development check and February–December 2024 check were rerun under `../results/v3_eia_*`. Historical frames and schedules are retained as erroneous version-2 artifacts for audit, not used in the version-3 tables. `../audit/v3_eia_unit_correction.json` records source and corrected hashes.

The EIA CSVs change their solar/wind field naming in July--December 2024 to split generation with and without integrated storage. The final parser combines the reported component categories, excluding imputed and adjusted variants. A post-freeze parser amendment records the change. The prior source-year file exposed seven 2024-01-01 UTC input rows without CO2, so January 2024 was prospectively excluded from the final sample. No outcome was selected using those rows.

An official [EPA AVERT Texas 2023 resource](https://www.epa.gov/avert/download-avert) was also acquired as a candidate. Its AVERT region and marginal-displacement model are not identical to the EIA `ERCO` balancing-area and production-emission boundary, so it was not silently spliced into the main response series. The public-data check should be interpreted as regional-input stress testing, **not independent causal validation** of emission reductions.
